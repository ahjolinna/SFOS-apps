Sailfish OS apps

14 unofficial apps for Sailfish OS 5, made for the Jolla Phone 2026 (aarch64, Sailfish OS 5.2). Ready-to-install RPM packages, with source RPMs for every one of them.

These apps were made with AI (Claude by Anthropic and Grok by xAI), with a human testing them on a real Jolla Phone 2026. Some are well used and quite usable, others are early or experimental: expect bugs and rough edges. Use at your own discretion. They are not in the Jolla Store and are not affiliated with Jolla or the services they connect to.

Installing

  1. Enable Developer mode on the phone (Settings → Developer tools), and allow untrusted software (Settings → Untrusted software) if you install by tapping the file.
  2. Download the app's RPM on the phone (and its add-on packages, if it has any).
  3. Tap the file in the file manager, or in a terminal: 
    devel-su pkcon install-local ~/Downloads/harbour-<app>-<version>.aarch64.rpm
    Install an app and its add-ons in the same command. To update, install the newer RPMs the same way.

Good to know

Apps marked unofficial use public or undocumented interfaces of the services they connect to, and can stop working whenever those services change.

Sailjail is Sailfish OS's app sandbox. Most of these apps run inside it with only the permissions listed on their card. Arena, Ludus and Tubus turn it off (each card says why): they can then read and write all your files, like any app outside the sandbox.

Other Sailfish OS 5 phones (aarch64) may work too, but the apps are only tested on the Jolla Phone 2026.

Source-available. Every RPM has a matching source RPM in the releases. Each contains the app's code, the shared code it uses and the sources of the bundled third-party libraries and emulators, and rebuilds on its own in the Sailfish OS SDK:

rpmbuild --rebuild harbour-<app>-<version>-1.src.rpm

Third-party parts keep their own licences (GPL, LGPL, MPL, and the non-commercial licences of snes9x and Genesis Plus GX).
